I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at [Millsaps College](https://www.millsaps.edu/) in Jackson, MS. My research interests lie in **functional analysis and operator theory**, with a focus on Banach space geometry, the structure of projections and isometries on Banach spaces, the numerical index, and the Bishop-Phelps-Bollobás property.

## Education

I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at the [University of Memphis](https://www.memphis.edu/msci/) in 2021 under the guidance of [Dr. Fernanda Botelho](https://www.memphis.edu/msci/people/mbotelho.php). I received my Master's degree in Mathematics from the [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Kolkata](https://math.iiserkol.ac.in/) and my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from the [University of Calcutta](https://www.caluniv.ac.in/).

Prior to joining Millsaps, I held postdoctoral positions at [Kenyon College](https://www.kenyon.edu/) (2023–2025), the [University of South Florida](https://www.usf.edu/) (2022–23), and [Virginia Tech](https://www.vt.edu/) (2021–22).

## Research

My research sits at the intersection of functional analysis, operator theory, and Banach space geometry. I am broadly interested in understanding how algebraic and spectral properties of operators on Banach spaces interact with the geometric structure of the space.

**Key research areas:**

- **Banach Space Geometry & Projections**: Hermitian projections, bicircular projections, and characterizations of projections in operator algebras
- **Numerical Index**: Convergence via operator openings, ultraproducts, and connections to Banach space structure  
- **Bishop-Phelps-Bollobás Property**: Extensions and variants, operators into spaces with extremal disconnectedness
- **N-potent Operators**: Spectral structure and characterizations of projections in algebras generated by n-potent operators
- **Isometries**: Surjective isometries and their role in Banach space geometry


## Teaching Philosophy

I believe in creating an active and inclusive classroom environment where students develop both mathematical intuition and the ability to think and communicate rigorously. I maintain a strong interest in undergraduate research and enjoy guiding students as they learn to ask good questions, work through uncertainty, and communicate mathematics with clarity and precision.
