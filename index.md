I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at [Millsaps College](https://www.millsaps.edu/) in Jackson, Mississippi. My research is in **functional analysis and operator theory**, with particular interests in Banach space geometry, projections and isometries on Banach spaces, the numerical index, and the Bishop-Phelps-Bollobás property.

I am also a [Project NExT Fellow](https://maa.org/maa-project-next/) in the **Silver ’26 cohort** of the Mathematical Association of America.

## Education

I earned my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the [University of Memphis](https://www.memphis.edu/msci/) in 2021 under the guidance of [Dr. Fernanda Botelho](https://www.memphis.edu/msci/people/mbotelho.php). I received my master's degree in Mathematics from the [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Kolkata](https://math.iiserkol.ac.in/) and my bachelor's degree in Mathematics from the [University of Calcutta](https://www.caluniv.ac.in/).

Prior to joining Millsaps, I held postdoctoral positions at [Kenyon College](https://www.kenyon.edu/) (2023–2025), the [University of South Florida](https://www.usf.edu/) (2022–23), and [Virginia Tech](https://www.vt.edu/) (2021–22).
