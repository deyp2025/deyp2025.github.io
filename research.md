My research lies at the intersection of functional analysis, operator theory, and the geometry of Banach spaces. I am particularly interested in how algebraic and spectral properties of operators reflect—and are shaped by—the geometry of the underlying space.

## Research Themes

### Isometries and Projections

I study structural connections between isometries and projections on Banach spaces. This includes understanding when projections can be represented using convex combinations of isometries or algebraic combinations of powers of finite-order operators.

### Numerical Index and Operator Geometry

The numerical index measures how the norm and numerical radius interact on a Banach space. My work considers convergence questions for the numerical index and related operator-theoretic constructions, including methods involving operator openings and ultraproducts.

### Bishop–Phelps–Bollobás Properties

I am interested in quantitative forms of norm attainment for operators. Recent work studies the Bishop–Phelps–Bollobás property for extremally disconnected ranges, with particular attention to separable and low-density domains.
